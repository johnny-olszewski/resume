I designed the SOPs and documentation that gave teams across product, engineering, data teams and Operations a single source of truth which turned experiment data into decisions stakeholders trust.

My Virginia Tech M.S.\ focused on healthcare data management and information systems. I understand how healthcare data flows, where it breaks down, and how to build governance processes that hold.

My career has required rapid mastery in high-stakes environments. From an aviation environment to healthcare engineering to product management, I have consistently delivered ahead of expectations in each transition.
