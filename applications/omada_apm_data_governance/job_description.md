# Associate Product Manager, Data Governance
**Omada Health** — Remote, USA

---

## Company

Omada Health (Nasdaq: OMDA) delivers virtual-first care for chronic conditions including obesity, diabetes, hypertension, cholesterol, and musculoskeletal conditions. They combine human-led care teams, connected devices, and AI-enabled technology to deliver personalized care at scale. Omada has served over 2 million members across 2,000+ employers, health plans, pharmacy benefit managers, and health systems.

---

## Role Overview

Enterprise-level owner of analytics governance, shared definitions, and enablement processes. Ensures Omada can answer critical business questions faster and more efficiently. Spans Product, Clinical, Commercial, Operations, and Finance.

---

## Responsibilities

- Establish and maintain authoritative definitions for core business and clinical concepts; resolve conflicts between local and enterprise views
- Design and evolve governance processes, SOPs, and engagement models enabling efficient analyst and business user workflows
- Collaborate with product managers to incorporate data science concepts into product features and user experiences
- Act as central product owner for Omada's analytics ecosystem, setting standards and driving adoption company-wide

---

## Requirements

- 5+ years relevant experience with Bachelor's degree (or 3+ years with advanced degree)
- Subject matter expertise in enterprise analytics governance, data product management, or analytics enablement
- Demonstrated experience establishing governance processes and tool implementation for data definitions, metrics, or analytics across multiple business functions
- Experience solving poorly-defined problems using advanced knowledge and conceptual thinking
- Healthcare data standards and regulatory requirements experience strongly preferred

---

## Competencies

**Strategic & Analytical**
- Identify new data applications impacting members and customers
- Strong analytical skills with solid technical understanding
- Ability to analyze data and lead research/discovery efforts

**Communication & Influence**
- Exceptional written and verbal communication
- Build formal networks with decision makers; influence stakeholders across functions
- Adapt communication style for technical teams through executives

**Collaboration & Problem Solving**
- Comfort with ambiguity; solve problems with limited resources and information
- Reconcile opposing stakeholder views to drive results

**Execution & Delivery**
- Consistent track record delivering against target metrics
- Autonomous decision-making with limited methodological guidance
