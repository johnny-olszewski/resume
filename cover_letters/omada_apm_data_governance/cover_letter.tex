Dear Omada Health Hiring Team,

Effective analytics governance is a prerequisite for data-driven product decisions — yet most organizations struggle to build it because they treat it as a technical problem rather than a product problem. The work requires establishing shared definitions, designing durable processes, and enabling cross-functional teams to trust the data they act on. That is the work I am doing today at ResMed, and it is what drew me to this role at Omada Health.

As PM on secondment at ResMed, I own product vision for a mobile experimentation platform enabling product teams to run A/B experiments that identify which features drive the best patient outcomes across a 3M+ active user population. Delivering reliable insights from that platform required a governance foundation first: authoritative definitions of active sessions, event attribution, and clinically meaningful signals. I designed the SOPs and documentation that gave teams across Product, Clinical, Engineering, and Operations a single source of truth — turning experiment data into decisions stakeholders trust.

My background is well-suited for this scope. As an iOS engineer at ResMed, I shipped features under HIPAA constraints and integrated real-time data pipelines between mobile clients, hardware, and AWS services. My Virginia Tech M.S.\ focused on healthcare data management and information systems — curriculum covering EHR standards, health database architecture, and enterprise data integration across clinical stakeholders. I understand how healthcare data flows, where it breaks down, and how to build governance processes that hold.

My career has required rapid mastery in high-stakes environments — from aviation systems to healthcare engineering to product management — and I have consistently delivered ahead of expectations in each transition. I pick up new domains quickly, ask the right questions early, and build credibility with technical and non-technical stakeholders alike. I am confident I can do the same at Omada Health.

Omada's mission to make evidence-based care scalable is precisely the kind of problem where governance is a product differentiator, not an afterthought. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute.
